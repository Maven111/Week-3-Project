The Problem this product solves:

People often own valuable items they no longer need while wanting other items they cannot or do not want to pay for entirely with cash.

Buy by Barter
> Trade what you have for what you want.

Buy by Barter is a free platform that allows people to exchange products they already own for products they want.

Instead of selling an item first and then using the money to buy another item, users can offer what they already have in exchange for something they need.

Users can also combine multiple items and add cash to make an offer.

---

Here's How It Works

1. List what you have

Add an item you want to trade.

You can provide:

- Product name
- Brand and model
- Condition
- Photos
- Description
- Location
- Estimated value
- What you want in exchange

2. Find what you want

Browse or search for items listed by other users.

You can search by:

- Product
- Brand
- Category
- Location
- Condition
- Estimated value

3. Make an offer

Choose one or more items from your listings and offer them for another user's item.

For example:

**You offer:**

- iPhone 11
- PlayStation 4
- ₦100,000 cash

**You want:**

- MacBook Air

### 4. Negotiate

The other user can:

- Accept your offer
- Reject your offer
- Send a counter offer

5. Complete the exchange

Once both users agree, they arrange the physical exchange themselves.

Both users confirm when the exchange is complete.

---

Multi-Item Barter

Buy by Barter supports more than a simple one-item-for-one-item exchange.

You can combine multiple items you own to make an offer.

For example:

```text
